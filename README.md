# customer-retention-credit-risk-analytics
An end-to-end customer churn analytics project using Excel, PostgreSQL, and Tableau. The project focuses on data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, churn behavior analysis, credit score intelligence, and customer retention insights for a banking dataset.

# Bank Customer Intelligence & Churn Analytics

This project analyzes customer churn behavior in a banking environment using Excel, PostgreSQL, and Tableau.

The objective is to identify churn drivers, customer risk segments, credit score patterns, and retention opportunities through interactive dashboards and business insights.

## Business Problem

Customer attrition directly impacts revenue and customer lifetime value.

The bank wants to:

- Identify high-risk customers
- Understand churn behavior
- Analyze credit score impact
- Improve customer retention
- Reduce revenue loss
